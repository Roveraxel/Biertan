# Burnt Vineyards, Gorge and Quarry — Environment Art Brief

## Existing source areas

Inspect `scripts/vineyard_level.gd` and `scripts/gorge_level.gd` in full before implementation. The story arc includes the burnt vineyards, strigoi churchyard/bats and the three-headed Balaur encounter at the Drowned Quarry. Preserve all existing bounds, routes, boss arena, water and encounter positions.

## Burnt Vineyards

Visual identity: late-autumn Transylvanian vineyard after fire and neglect. Use dry vine rows, charred stakes, scorched earth, brittle leaves, collapsed trellis sections, ash drifts and occasional surviving green growth. Keep rows aligned with the level's actual layout and avoid making them collision barriers unless the source already defines collision.

Asset kit: vine-row modules, charred posts, dry vine clusters, scorched ground patches, ash, broken baskets, grape press/barrel details, low stone boundaries and cemetery details where present.

## Gorge / Drowned Quarry

Visual identity: exposed quarry rock, steep walls, damp stone, dark water and industrial traces of extraction. Use layered rock faces, scree, broken timber, mineral seams, water edges, submerged stone and abandoned quarry equipment. The boss arena should remain visually uncluttered around the Balaur's attack lanes.

Asset kit: rock wall segments, cliff edge transitions, gravel/scree, quarry timber, broken winch/cart parts, water edge tiles, foam/ripple overlays, mineral deposits and large boulder silhouettes.

## Acceptance

Both areas have distinct materials and atmosphere. Preserve combat lanes, enemy readability, route continuity, water boundaries, triggers and boss behaviour.
