# Biertan

A Hunter's Ballad of Transylvania: a top-down action RPG inspired by Romanian folk mythology (Godot 4.3).

**Play:** open this folder in Godot 4.3 and press F5.
**Controls:** WASD move · mouse aim + click attack · Space dodge · E interact · Q garlic tincture.

## Level 1 (vertical slice)
Saschiz (hub) → Wolf Forest → the Varcolac in the Moon Clearing.

## Assets
All art and audio are original and generated by scripts, so there is nothing to license:
- `tools/gen_sprites.py` writes NPC, scarecrow, shade and Varcolac SVGs (the Wanderer, wolf and tile SVGs were authored in Claude Design).
- `tools/gen_audio.py` synthesizes sound effects, ambience and music loops (needs numpy and scipy).

## Tests
`godot --headless --path . -- --autotest` plays through the whole slice and prints `TEST RESULT: OK`.
