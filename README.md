# Biertan

A Hunter's Ballad of Transylvania: a top-down action RPG inspired by Romanian folk mythology (Godot 4.3).

**Play:** https://roveraxel.github.io/Biertan/ (phones too, in landscape) or open this folder in Godot 4.3 and press F5.
**Controls:** WASD move · mouse aim + click attack · Space dodge · E interact · Q garlic tincture · R throw salt. The game saves when you reach a village; pick Continue on the title screen.

## Levels
1. Saschiz (hub) → Wolf Forest → the Varcolac in the Moon Clearing.
2. Richiș (hub) → Hollow Hills (Iele fairy rings) → Salt Caves (moroi, salt crystals) → Muma Pădurii in the Hollow.
3. Moșna (hub) → Burnt Vineyards (strigoi churchyard, bats) → the three-headed Balaur in the Drowned Quarry.
4. Biertan (survivors in the Marriage Prison) → the Three Walls (blood wards seal each gate) → Strigoi Vlad in the church; ring the great bell. Ending and credits.

## Extras
- Optional master: the Bear of the Garden (Grădina Ursului, from Harap-Alb), hidden in the south-east of the Hollow Hills.
- Wolf Forest side quests: Gheorghe's lost ewes, the hermit's spring, and haiduc treasure that only burns blue at midnight.
- Bestiary (title menu) with the real folklore behind every creature and character.

## Assets
Art and audio are generated by scripts, except the photos:
- `assets/photos/`: village photos from Wikimedia Commons (authors and licences in `credits.json`, shown in game), fetched by `tools/fetch_photos.py`.
- `assets/art/`: the title and Biertan story image, from a photo by the project owner.
- `tools/gen_sprites.py` writes NPC, scarecrow, shade, Iele, moroi, strigoi, bat, Varcolac, Muma Pădurii, Balaur and Vlad SVGs (the Wanderer, wolf and tile SVGs were authored in Claude Design).
- `tools/gen_audio.py` synthesizes sound effects, ambience and music loops (needs numpy and scipy; ffmpeg compresses the loops to Ogg).

## Tests
`godot --headless --path . -- --autotest` plays through the whole game to the ending and prints `TEST RESULT: OK`.
