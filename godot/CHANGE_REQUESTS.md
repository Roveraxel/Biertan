## 1. Critical Collision, Gates & Progression

- **[06:08–08:02] Village Gate & Church Collision (Sighișoara):**
  - Village gate blocks view / is unreachable behind props (`06:08–07:44`).
  - Player can phase straight through the church (`06:44`).
  - Sexton NPC walks through walls like a ghost (`07:55–08:02`).
- **[10:15–10:33] Cellar Iron Gate (Sighișoara):**
  - Iron gate in front of Matei is oversized, sits in the middle of the room instead of the entrance, and can be walked straight through.
  - Should block passage until unlocked (e.g., require a key from the Sexton or open properly via interaction).
- **[12:35–12:46] Road South to Criț Blocked by Stone Wall:**
  - A solid stone wall blocks the road where a gate used to be; the player currently just teleports through the wall. Restore a proper gate/opening.
- **[29:47–29:55] Saschiz Fortress Entrance:**
  - Fortress entrance appears closed/inaccessible.
- **[17:50] Old Cemetery Fence Gaps:**
  - Wooden fence segments around the old cemetery have wide gaps and do not connect cleanly.

## 3. Art Direction, Palette & Architecture

- **[01:48–02:20] Cohesive Design System:**
  - Establish a unified visual design system so props, buildings, trees, and textures do not look pieced together from mismatched asset packs.
- **[01:05–01:19, 09:28–09:39] Sighișoara Cellar Palette & Props:**
  - Floor looks overly red/cracked ("Wind Hill" look).
  - Bright green jars/bottles on the cellar table and colorful bookshelf clash sharply with the cellar palette; replace with custom in-theme jars/props.
- **[13:28–13:32, 15:16–15:27] Criț Houses:**
  - Stark bright-white houses next to pitch-black houses create harsh contrast.
  - Several houses in Criț look unnaturally squished/thin (`15:16–15:27`).
- **[24:32–25:14, 27:06–27:20] Saschiz Visuals:**
  - Untextured flat white/beige boxes, white/pink ducks along the road, and isolated grey stone blocks lack visual cohesion.
  - Tiny flat pixel-square grass flowers (`27:06–27:20`) should look more realistic.
- **[28:07–28:39] Uriași (Giant) Fight Visuals:**
  - Giant's thrown boulder is just a white circle — give it a proper rock sprite/design.
  - Fortress courtyard is too barren/empty.
- **[33:05–33:27, 35:35–35:39, 36:00–36:28] Șapartoc / Biertan Scale & Density:**
  - Make Radu's inn larger with outdoor garden/seating (`33:05–33:27`).
  - Fill sparse/empty town spacing with more interactive elements (`35:35–35:39`).
  - Villagers/priest scale is too large (`36:00–36:10`).
  - Waking priest suddenly gains hair/gray head mismatching his sleeping sprite (`36:10–36:28`).
- **[38:59–39:38] Apold Autumn Setting & Church:**
  - Fall setting should not have fields of spring flowers.
  - Church structure is overly dark/bare and needs architectural detail.
- **[02:42–03:00] Sighișoara Content Density:**
  - City is large; add more content and activities to fill it out.

## 5. Combat, Quest UX & Traversal

- **[11:48, 40:14, 40:58–41:30] Map Traversal & Shift-to-Run:**
  - Too much walking across large maps (`41:15–41:30`, even after map shrinking).
  - Matei follows too slowly (`11:48`).
  - Add a **Shift-to-run** mechanic with a stamina bar so players do not have to spam Space-dash (`40:58–41:11`).
- **[13:50–14:08] Criț Quest Clarity:**
  - Quest objective/flow in Criț was confusing (unclear what the player just did or is supposed to do).
- **[22:33–22:48] Saschiz Road Quest Pointer:**
  - Pointer aims backwards when approaching from the north instead of following the road.
- **[28:21] Uriași Camera Shake:**
  - Reduce camera shake intensity during the Giant encounter.
- **[42:17–44:57] Apold Cemetery Wave Combat:**
  - Restore the **percentage remaining on the food/offering table** UI (`42:49–42:52`).
  - Nightfall objective is confusing (`42:17–42:34`: points to wood pile / lighting wood fire without clear reason, and carrying *colivă* to the table at `43:28–43:32` feels annoying).
  - Ghosts swarm from all around instead of from the cemetery (`43:21–43:25`), and attack grave food away from the main table (`44:57`), making it unclear what to defend.
  - **Pause ghost movement** while wave/story notifications are displayed (`44:43–44:47`).
