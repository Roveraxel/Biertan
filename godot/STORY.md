# Biertan: story plan (agreed with Alex, 14 Nov 2026)

Status: all three acts are built and play end to end for the bot (tests/bot.gd). Act 3 levels: gorge, vineyard, forest, tunnel, viscri (taken), sighisoara (finale, FinQuest), hillchurch. Open: Joimărița's sprite to be redrawn young (mid-twenties, flowers in her hair); hand playtest of every fight.
Dropped for good: hub, old Richiș, caves, hollow, Moșna, old Biertan, walls, church, hills.
Kept and reused in act 3: gorge, vineyard, Wolf Forest, arena (Moon Clearing).

## Who is who
- **The baron of Biertan.** A young Saxon of good family who died the night before his wedding in 1515. Biertan
  denied him the wedding of the dead, so he walks as a strigoi mort and has played lord of the village ever since.
  Tired, courteous, half unwilling. He cannot die in this story.
- **The sexton** (the Viscri sexton, who also keeps the Biertan church door: two bells, two salaries). In the Viscri
  crypt he only plays spellbound when he is caught (tells: clean hands, new boots, Matei saw him counting bags);
  he mocks the hunter for believing it at the wedding. The real villain, acting from self-interest. He opened the sacristy
  door and carries grave earth from the baron's resting place out in small bags, leaving it where it does harm
  (wolf dens, the Iele ridge, the Mălâncrav crypt, the Viscri barn), then sells the frightened villages protection.
  The baron does not know. This grave earth is "the rot".
- **Lorena.** Taken by the baron as the bride he never had. Not a victim: she bit his finger off and tried to stuff
  garlic in his mouth. Annoyed that the hunter took so long, and halfway through her own escape when he arrives.
- **Magda and Vlad Laurențiu.** A couple with the cart. They flirt by bickering: she tells him he is slow, he answers
  calmly and makes her laugh against her will. Standing pose: facing each other, hands on hips, half smiling
  (after the Bregenzerwald photo Alex sent). They recur along the road, always arrived first; the cart carries the
  shop stall. Vlad delivers to the sexton and recognises the bags.
- **The spiriduși** of the Butchers' Tower. Allies at the end.
- **Azorel.** Hurt but on his feet in Viscri in act 3; comes along from there.

## Act 1: the road to Biertan (built)
Stână, Sighișoara, Viscri, Criț, Saschiz, Șapartoc, Apold, Criș, Mălâncrav, Richiș. Additions to make:
- A small bag of dark earth as an unexplained find in three or four villages.
- Sighișoara gives a ninth token: a bent tin button from the spiriduși, "for when you come back".
- Three or four short notes from Lorena left at troițe by Vlad's cart: dry, practical, slightly rude about the
  hunter's progress. The last one goes unanswered.
- Magda and Vlad appear in every second or third village.

## Act 2: Biertan
**By day.** Quest "The Baron's Table". Market in the street, a warm welcome, the baron invites the hunter to supper.
Observation: no salt on the table, no garlic in the kitchen, he pours wine and never drinks, grey-faced sleepy
servants, no dogs anywhere, the bell never rung, he is never seen in church. Investigation through the three walls:
- Outer: the marriage prison couple; nobody has married in Biertan for years, the baron "does not care for weddings".
- Middle: the graveyard, one plot dug over again and again; Vlad recognises the bags.
- Inner: the sexton's house, bags to the ceiling and a ledger of what each village paid him.
It ends when the hunter says aloud what the baron is. The sexton hears first.

**By night.** The sexton empties every bag into the streets and the bell rings the alarm. Villagers flee down the
covered stair, Magda and Vlad loading children on the cart. The fortress fills: rot-eyed wolves in the outer ring,
moroi and strigoi among the graves in the middle ring, the baron's servants as thralls in the inner ring with the
sexton commanding until he slips away. Fight up through the three gates (the level as built). The marriage prison
couple refuse to flee and argue inside the barricaded prison.
At the top the sacristy door of 1515, bolted from inside: nine tokens draw eighteen bolts, the baron opens the
nineteenth himself ("You took your time. Supper is cold."). Fight: the lord with the bell rope, wolves and bats.
No stake here. He breaks apart and flees down the tunnel.

## Act 3: the chase back
1. **Cart** with Magda and Vlad as far as the road goes.
2. **Gorge: the Solomonar.** He sees the hunter come from Biertan (which refused him bread) and curses him: hail and
   wind. He calls his balaur down; fight in the storm, balaur beaten back, never killed. Then the hunter shares his
   bread, the Solomonar sees his mistake, lifts the curse and owes a favour.
3. **Vineyard: Marțolea.** Late harvest, women working Tuesday night in the press-house, where the groom's wedding
   shirt is being sewn. Before dark: turn every pot, bucket, trough and lamp face down, broom across the threshold
   (observation task). She knocks, calls the objects by name; each one missed answers and makes trouble. The fight is
   as hard as the number missed. A Criț grandmother explains the rule. She leaves at cockcrow.
4. **Wolf Forest into the Moon Clearing: Joimărița.** Wolves and other creatures; a faint smell of spring flowers,
   pockets of snowdrops, the hunter notes they are very late. Boss: the vârcolac, who eats the moon (forest goes
   dark). Beaten, the moon returns and a spring glade opens. Joimărița, held captive since spring, inspects the
   hunter's hands first, then thanks him.
5. **Tunnel: Cățelul pământului.** Heard barking underground since Biertan. A small pale dog who walks ahead, barks
   at unsafe floor and at buried bags of earth, and will not pass until each is dug up. Guide, never an enemy.
6. **Viscri.** Lorena's gate open, house turned over, Azorel hurt but standing. He joins.
7. **Sighișoara.**
   - The spiriduși know the button, say which real tower Lorena is in (e.g. the Tinsmiths' Tower) and show the back
     way. Lorena hung it with garlic from the inside.
   - The meeting. Hunter: free her at once, and if she has suffered your whole line will regret it. Baron (bandaged
     hand): "Who, that girl? She is wild. She bit my finger off and tried to stuff garlic in my mouth. I had to lock
     her in the tower. Here, take the key. Just keep her away from me."
   - He admits he is tired and warns that at midnight he is not himself.
   - **Midnight fight.** The strigoi takes over. Bell mechanic; the goal is to hold him until first light, not kill.
   - **The funeral-wedding.** Hunter and Lorena prepare the wedding of the dead: fir tree for the bride's place, the
     groom's shirt from the vineyard, the drunk Hungarian priest from Șapartoc. Joimărița comes to inspect the work
     and the two of them flirt in the background. Lorena notices first.
   - **Cold feet.** At the open coffin he changes his mind and asks the priest to marry him to Joimărița. She checks
     his hands.
   - **Defend the wedding.** Saxon cemetery by the Church on the Hill. The sexton raises the dead and brings the last
     rot-touched wolves. Waves like the Apold night, sexton as final boss. Allies: Muma's bear and animals, the
     spiriduși (who also cater, with Meister Thomas's bacon), Azorel, Lorena with garlic, the Solomonar's hail,
     Magda shouting the dead off the tables, Cățelul pământului sending the dead back down.
   - **The sexton's end.** Joimărița inspects his hands, finds he never did an honest day's work, burns his fingers
     and sets him to spin hemp until Maundy Thursday.
   - **Ending.** The hunter plants Muma's oak stake as his gift and it leafs out at the vows. The couple leaves with
     the spring; snowdrops come up along the road. Three couples: Magda and Vlad arguing, the baron and Joimărița,
     Lorena and the hunter. Lorena finally asks about the trousers.

## Folklore notes
- Unmarried dead walk; the wedding of the dead (fir tree as bride) settles them.
- A strigoi's power sits in his grave earth.
- Solomonar: beggar, book and staff, balaur, hail on those who refuse bread.
- Marțolea: Tuesday evening, upturned vessels cannot answer her.
- Joimărița: Maundy Thursday, inspects spinning, burns lazy hands. Popular belief makes her an ugly old woman;
  in this game (Alex's decision) she is the spirit of spring: mid-twenties, a free spirit, flowers in her hair,
  barefoot. Still frightening when she inspects hands.
- Vârcolac: eats the moon. Cățelul pământului: underground near graves, barks as a warning.
- Sources to use: Pamfile, Coman, Cosma. Not Adevărul, Newsweek or PressOne.
