## 1. The Criț Subquests

### Subquest A: "The Distaff in the Millrace" (*Furca din morișcă*)
- **Location:** The watermill stream behind Floarea's garden, Criț.
- **NPCs:** Anica (young Saxon miller's apprentice), Nea Toma (the distiller).
- **Steps:**
  1. Anica stands weeping by the churning millwheel: she dropped her carved walnut distaff into the stream on Thursday dusk while rushing to beat the Friday work taboo.
  2. If unspun flax soaks in running water on Friday, folklore warns the water-spirit (*Fata Pădurii* / *Știma apei*) will weave a drowning-net.
  3. The hunter wades into the cold sluice gate, dodges the spinning paddle blades, and fishes out the distaff.
  4. Bring it to Nea Toma's copper still to dry by the fire and rub it with protective salt.
- **Reward:** 15 coins and a flask of strong double-distilled plum țuică (restores 40 stamina).
- **Key Dialogue:**
  - *Anica:* "It slipped right out of my apron into the foam! If the water-maid takes the flax, she spins a shroud before morning."
  - *Hunter:* "(You look at the black water under the wheel. Then at your good boots.)"
  - *Anica:* "Don't just stand there making faces at the current! A shepherd isn't made of sugar; you won't melt."
  - *Nea Toma:* "(He takes the dripping distaff, smells the wool, and sets it on the still's warm copper hood.)"
  - *Nea Toma:* "Salt and plum steam. If any river demon had her teeth in this, she's spitting gravel by now."
  - *Lorena:* "(from the fence) Look at you. Dripping like a drowned sheep, and smelling of boiled plums. Quite the hero."

### Subquest B: "The Red Thread on the Well" (*Ața roșie de la fântână*)
- **Location:** The village draw-well by the church lane, Criț.
- **NPCs:** Baba Safta.
- **Steps:**
  1. Baba Safta is angrily waving a skein of sheep's wool dyed red with madder root: the village well has gone cloudy because someone looked into it without spitting three times against the evil eye (*deochi*).
  2. She orders the hunter to tie three double-knots of red wool around the wooden windlass beam.
  3. Simple mini-task: interact with the beam three times; each knot requires passing her inspection.
  4. Pour a cup of fresh well water into a brass bowl: the water settles crystal clear.
- **Reward:** A bundle of dried St. John's wort (*sunătoare*) and 10 coins.
- **Key Dialogue:**
  - *Baba Safta:* "Some fool with yellow eyes stared straight down the shaft this morning. Never spat, never crossed himself. Now the bucket comes up tasting of sour silt."
  - *Hunter:* "It's just clay from the rain, mother."
  - *Baba Safta:* "Clay! Hear the clever mountain boy! Tie the wool, master philosopher, and tie it tight. Three knots, left over right."
  - *Hunter:* "(You tie the first knot. It looks like an old rope on a goat.)"
  - *Baba Safta:* "A calf could tie better with its tail. Undo it! Pull from the thumb side!"
  - *Baba Safta:* "(She drops a pebble down. A clear, deep chime answers.) There. The eye is blinded. Drink, before you die of being proud."

## 2. A Scene Where Village Children Sing

- **Location:** The churchyard steps of Saschiz, beneath the great clock tower in late afternoon sun.
- **NPCs:** Paul, Ioana, Andrei, and four village children.
- **Steps:**
  1. After the giant returns the three children, the village children gather on the stone steps to sing the ancient sun-calling chant (*Cântecul Soarelui* / *Ieși, soare*) to keep shadows from creeping down the fortress hill.
  2. The children notice the hunter leaning against the stone parapet, cleaning his blade.
  3. They form a skipping circle around him, clapping rhythmically and adapting the folk verses to mock his oversized hat, his scarred leather vest, and his silent Carpathian dog.
  4. The hunter can offer them dried plum wedges or whistle along on his willow pipe (*fluier*).
- **Reward:** A braided wreath of dry meadow sweetgrass (*iarba fiarelor* token of goodwill, raises village reputation).
- **Key Dialogue (Children's Song):**
  - *Children (singing):*
    "Ieși, soare, din închisoare,
    Că te caută o căprioară,
    Cu cercei de aur mare!
    Vino, soare, pe pământ,
    Că la noi e numai vânt!"
  - *Ioana:* "(She points at the hunter.) Look at the big shepherd! His dog has nicer manners than he does!"
  - *Paul (skipping):*
    "Big boots, long knife,
    Afraid of his own wife!
    Came from the mountain, slept in the hay,
    Chased all the giant's sheep away!"
  - *Hunter:* "(You reach into your pack, find three dried plums, and hold them out on your palm.)"
  - *Andrei:* "(He grabs a plum, inspects it for dirt, wipes it on his knee, and bites.) ...They're sweet. He's allowed to stay."
  - *Lorena:* "(leaning on the low wall) Don't look so offended, hunter. Children and drunkards: the only two kinds of people who speak the plain truth."

## 3. Sfânta Vineri: In-Story Explanation

- **Location:** The sacred clearing at `critforest`, by the shingled oak shrine.
- **NPCs:** Sfânta Vineri (an ascetic, ancient holy woman clothed in lichen-grey homespun, barefoot on moss), Floarea.
- **Steps:**
  1. Floarea kneels at the shrine with her raw, cracked hands; Sfânta Vineri appears not as a demonic monster nor a sweet saint, but as the severe, primordial keeper of sacred time (*Vinerile oprite*).
  2. Sfânta Vineri explains why Friday cannot be worked: Friday is the rest of the soil, the breath between the seed and the leaf. When humans spin, sew, or hammer on her day, they pierce the earth's skin and invite frost and locusts.
  3. The hunter must place the offering basket on the stone altar without looking directly into her face.
  4. She blesses the oil, touches Floarea's wrists, and issues a stern warning to the hunter about his own road to Biertan.
- **Reward:** Floarea's hands heal; hunter receives the holy ribbon of Friday (*token_ribbon*).
- **Key Dialogue:**
  - *Sfânta Vineri:* "You call me a saint in your stone churches, and a witch when you whisper by the chimney. I am neither. I am Friday."
  - *Hunter:* "The girl only wanted to finish her bridal sheets, mother."
  - *Sfânta Vineri:* "Six days the wheel may scream and the needle bite. On the seventh, the earth must sleep. Who works on Friday tears my veil. If I do not burn the fingers, the hail burns the whole valley."
  - *Floarea:* "(whispering through tears) Forgive me, grandmother. I was proud. I wanted three skirts instead of two."
  - *Sfânta Vineri:* "(She takes a drop of elderberry oil from the basket and touches Floarea's palms.) The skin remembers. The pride forgets. Go home, child. Wear homespun on your wedding day, and let the wheel sit cold when Thursday dusk falls."
  - *Sfânta Vineri:* "(turning her pale, ancient eyes on the hunter) And you, who carry an oak stake for a bridegroom who will not die: mind your own hours. Midnight in Biertan belongs to nobody living."

## 4. The Mushroom and the Turks (*Bureții turcului*)

- **Location:** The wooded hollow north of Șapartoc, by an old collapsed trench (*Șanțul Tătarilor*).
- **NPCs:** Moș Costache (old forager with a willow creel), Radu the innkeeper.
- **Steps:**
  1. Moș Costache sits by the path swearing at an anthill: he is looking for *bureți iuți* (peppery milky caps, *Lactarius piperatus*) for Radu's soup kettle, but his knees are stiff.
  2. He recounts the legend of how seventy years ago, an Ottoman raiding detachment camped in this hollow and stole four sacks of these wild white mushrooms, mistaking them for sweet field caps.
  3. The mushrooms are intensely peppery and caustic unless boiled three times in salted whey; the raiders ate them fried in tallow, developed violent belly-fire, and fled the valley thinking the Saxon church had poisoned the very air.
  4. The hunter gathers 6 pepper-caps from under the beech leaf-litter and returns them to Costache.
- **Reward:** 20 coins, a bowl of pepper-mushroom stew (boosts attack damage for 60 seconds).
- **Key Dialogue:**
  - *Moș Costache:* "White as lard on top, gills like fine paper underneath. But bite one raw, and your tongue curls up like dried boot leather."
  - *Hunter:* "Then why are we picking them for dinner?"
  - *Moș Costache:* "Because Radu has whey, boy! Boil them in salt whey, and they turn sweeter than veal. The Pasha's riders didn't know that. They fried three basketfuls over sheep fat and swallowed them down with sour wine."
  - *Hunter:* "And what happened?"
  - *Moș Costache:* "By midnight fifty Janissaries were rolling in the ditch shouting that the Virgin Mary had put live coals in their bellies! They left their saddles, their kettles, and three brass drums, and ran all the way to Sighișoara."
  - *Lorena:* "(chuckling) Make sure you tell Radu to boil them three times, hunter. I'd rather face thirty strigoi than your face after bad mushrooms."

## 5. The Saschiz Market

- **Location:** The triangular marketplace below the Saschiz fortified church tower.
- **NPCs:** Meister Thomas (visiting stall), Ilinca the potter, Vlad & Magda, Saxon wool-trader Jürgen.
- **Steps:**
  1. Market day in Saschiz: stalls are crowded with salt crates, red-glazed pots, wool blankets, and smoked sausage.
  2. A minor uproar erupts: trader Jürgen's runaway speckled piglet has bolted through the market rows, knocked over Ilinca's bench of unbaked clay pitchers, and wedged itself under Vlad's cart.
  3. Jürgen accuses Ilinca of leaving pots in the road; Ilinca accuses Jürgen of raising wild boars; Magda is standing on the cart seat threatening both with an iron ladle.
  4. The hunter uses Azorel to herd the squealing piglet out from under the axle into a wicker basket without harming it.
  5. Inspect the dispute: find Ilinca's broken marker peg showing Jürgen set his stall two cubits past his guild line.
- **Reward:** 30 coins, a fine red clay mug (*cănărușă de Saschiz*).
- **Key Dialogue:**
  - *Magda:* "Vlad, get down there! That pig is chewing through our bacon sack! I will crack its head with this ladle, I swear by Saint Anne!"
  - *Vlad:* "(sitting placidly on the shaft) It’s a very small pig, Magda. It has little teeth."
  - *Magda:* "It has TEETH ENOUGH for forty florins of cured ham! Move your carcass!"
  - *Ilinca:* "(holding a shattered red jar) Thirty hours on the wheel! Stamped with the guild fish! Jürgen, your sow will pay for this in lard!"
  - *Jürgen:* "Your pots were in the cart lane! The street belongs to the town council, not the potter's guild!"
  - *Hunter:* "(You whistle once. Azorel slips under the cart, nudges the piglet by the flank, and traps it in the empty flour crate. You pick up the surveyor's peg from the dirt.)"
  - *Hunter:* "The peg says the stall ends here, Meister Jürgen. Two paces back."
  - *Jürgen:* "(He coughs into his beard, glances at the crowd, and pulls his sacks back.) Hm. The peg must have... shifted in the mud."
  - *Meister Thomas:* "(laughing from his stall) Well judged, shepherd! You have a sharper eye for boundaries than the town magistrate."
